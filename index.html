use the search box

One quick thing before you install.

One tick and you’re good to go.

Tick the box first.

windows

  1. Press copy below.
  2. Press the Windows key, type PowerShell and press Enter. A blue or black window opens.
  3. Right-click inside it to paste, then press Enter. Zarp installs by itself and asks which folders to read.

This is the official installer for Zarp. It checks the download before installing, and no Windows warnings pop up.

Rather download a normal installer?

Download Zarp for Windows and open it. Windows may show a blue box saying it protected your PC. That’s normal for new apps from small makers: click More info, then Run anyway.

mac

  1. Press copy below.
  2. Press Cmd+Space, type Terminal and press Enter.
  3. Press Cmd+V to paste, then press Enter. Zarp goes into Applications, opens and asks which folders to read.

For Macs with an M1 chip or newer. It checks the download first, and there’s no “can’t verify the app” step.

Rather download it yourself?

Download Zarp for Mac and drag Zarp Beta into Applications. The first time, your Mac says it can’t check the app: open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Only once.

Then: pick your folders and press Start. Using the Claude or ChatGPT app? Quit it fully (right-click its icon near the clock, or Cmd+Q on a Mac) and open it again so it sees Zarp. On Claude Code, start a new session.

First thing to try: “Using Zarp, what do my notes say about a topic from your notes? Give the file and page.” Or skip the AI and press Ctrl+Alt+Space (on a Mac: Zarp icon at the top, then Search).

Want your data deleted? In Zarp’s menu choose Copy my tester ID and send it to Ishaan, or open a GitHub issue with it. Uninstalling stops all sending.
