Your AI, straight to the right page. At a quarter of the tokens.

Stop uploading the same PDFs over and over. WarpSearch learns your notes, slides and docs once, on your laptop, then hands Claude or Codex the exact page that answers your question. In our tests Claude got every answer right using about 4x fewer tokens, and it finished faster too. If something isn't in your notes, it tells you instead of making it up.

130 MB · no admin needed · updates itself

Two minutes to set up

  1. Run the installer. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway (beta builds aren't signed yet).
  2. Pick the folders with your notes and press Start.
  3. Quit Claude Desktop from the tray and open it again. On Claude Code or Codex, just start a new session.
  4. Ask: "Using WarpSearch, what did my notes say about <topic>? Cite the page."

Why you'll keep it

  • Your usage limits last way longer. Your assistant reads one page instead of whole files, so most of the tokens never get spent.
  • Answers in about 7 seconds instead of 11 in our tests, because there's no digging through files.
  • Every answer points to a file and page you can open and check.
  • It reads PDFs (scans too), Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Markdown, and picks up new files on its own.
  • Open "Activity" in the tray to see roughly how many tokens it has saved you.
