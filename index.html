windows, the easy way

  1. Press copy below.
  2. Press the Windows key, type PowerShell and press Enter. A blue or black window opens.
  3. Right-click inside it to paste, then press Enter.

It downloads Zarp, checks the download is the real one and installs it. No clicks, no Windows warning. Zarp then opens and asks which folders to read.

Prefer clicking? Download the .exe instead. Windows will probably say it protected your PC, because beta builds aren’t signed yet (a signing certificate costs money and the beta is free). Click More info, then Run anyway.

mac, the easy way

For Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer).

  1. Press copy below.
  2. Press Cmd+Space, type Terminal and press Enter.
  3. Press Cmd+V to paste, then press Enter.

It checks the download, puts Zarp in Applications and opens it, with no “can’t verify the app” detour.

Prefer clicking? Download the zip and drag Zarp Beta into Applications. The first time, your Mac says it can’t verify the app, because the beta isn’t notarized yet. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Then: pick your notes folders and press Start. Using Claude Desktop? Quit it fully (right-click its icon by the clock, or Cmd+Q on a Mac) and open it again so it sees Zarp. On Claude Code or Codex, start a new session.

First thing to try: “Using Zarp, what do my notes say about a topic from your notes? Give the file and page.” Or skip the AI and press Ctrl+Alt+Space (on a Mac: Zarp icon at the top, then Search).

Want your data deleted? Open a GitHub issue with your install ID. It’s in beta_settings.json inside %LOCALAPPDATA%\DiskAI-Beta on Windows, or ~/Library/Application Support/DiskAI-Beta on a Mac. Uninstalling stops all sending.
